### Notes for the implementing task and for Q7 (hand-written, `--check` ignores prose)

`dsh_webui/` must wait for Q7 because "the layout of its 66 files would change entirely". Measured:
there are **9 files** (a 4 280-line `src-tauri/src/dsh_webui.rs` plus 8 in `dsh_webui/`, 9 417 lines

equals the 66 `dsh_webui::` entries in `command-map.json`. The source-side row list is therefore
independent of Q7, and the part Q7 actually decides is narrower than assumed: how the 42/41 typed
methods are *exposed* in core, not where 9 files go.

**What the files actually are, which reframes Q7.** `remote_mux.rs` (990 lines) contains **no**
`#[tauri::command]` and does not dispatch a method-name table: its public surface is a client
(`connect`, `is_alive`, `open`, `next`, `remote_mux_url`, `parse_remote_event_item`,
`remote_event_result_args`) that talks to the DSH server over a mux URL. So the "typed commands are
bypassed by the mux" story is not one router replacing 42 methods; it is **two different surfaces
against an upstream-pinned protocol**: `protocol_inventory.rs` pins
`SOURCE_COMMIT c291e796…`, package `0.1.5-rc.2`, `PROTOCOL_VERSION 2`, and the 41 zero-evidence typed
commands are Aeroric's own Tauri-side wrappers over parts of that surface. Q7 is therefore mostly a
question of **who owns version skew with upstream dsh**, which is a different trade-off from
"42 methods vs 1 gateway" and should be re-asked on that basis.
